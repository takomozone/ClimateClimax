Interpretation

Correlation is a signal,
not a verdict.

01

Match by year

Annual ring widths are averaged across available cores and paired with climate values at the site.

02

Compare variation

Pearson’s r measures whether wider rings tend to occur in warmer or wetter years.

03

Check confounders

Tree age, competition, soil moisture, disturbances and seasonal climate can all affect growth.
